App Design Quality

"The app, while improved, still feels a bit behind in certain areas. Searching for a specific transaction by merchant name doesn't work very well — you can scroll through the list but there's no reliable search."

"The app is functional but it feels like it was designed five years ago and has had minimal investment since then."

"I find the overall UX a bit cluttered — there are so many products and sub-menus and it doesn't feel like the experience was designed with someone like me in mind."

"Compared to what I can see in the Revolut app, it feels quite unsophisticated for managing variable income."

"The app feels dated compared to what I'm used to from Wise or even Nationwide. Transaction labelling is poor — a payment from a client often just shows as some opaque reference code and I have to go hunting to figure out what it was."

Spending Visibility Through Categorization

"The app just shows you a flat view of transactions with no nuance. There's no way to see your cash flow over a rolling period — it's just a list of money in and money out."

"The app spending insights are quite weak. There's no way for me to see at a glance how my income this month compares to last month."

"I keep reading about banks where you can split your money into different pots, or get a real-time breakdown of what you've spent on food versus entertainment versus subscriptions, and none of that exists in my bank's app."

"There's no useful spending categorisation, so I have to do that work elsewhere."

"The app categorisation is really basic. It'll show me a total for the month but not any useful breakdown of where it's going. Friends who use newer apps seem to get much more useful insights from their banking app."

From interviews to strategic recommendations:
HR software example.

Three recommendations from the data

Marketing

Lead with "intuitive HR for growing companies."

BambooHR owns the "intuitive UI" lane at 70% concentration — the highest category-specific position in the dataset. No competitor comes close. Don't compete on platform breadth or feature parity. BambooHR wins by being the tool HR leaders actually enjoy using.

Product

Reporting is the universal unmet need.

85% of BambooHR customers cite reporting as a frustration. 62% say the platform falls short on reporting today. It surfaces as the top complaint in every analysis cut — current frustrations, biggest pain points, broader challenges. The single highest-impact product investment BambooHR can make.

Sales

Build a Gusto displacement playbook.

43% of Gusto users cite switching costs as the reason they stayed — retention by inertia, not satisfaction. A targeted migration program (dedicated specialist, data-transfer guarantee, 90-day parallel-run) could unlock BambooHR's single largest near-term revenue opportunity from competitive displacement.
